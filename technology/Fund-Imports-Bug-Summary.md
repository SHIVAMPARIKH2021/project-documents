## Bug Summary for QA Automation Regression Suite

### 1. Benchmark Resolution & Data Ingestion Bugs
##### **BUG-BM-01**: Ingestion of Form N-1A After-Tax Return Lines as Primary Benchmarks
   - **Root Cause**: In BenchmarkItemProcessor, the after-tax exclusion used strict ```java !lower.contains("aftertax")```. 
   - SEC XBRL filings contain space and plural variations (After Taxeson Distributionsand Salesof Shares, Return After Taxes on Distributions, Aftertaxondistributionswithsale), bypassing the filter and staging tax adjustments as index entities. ```java BenchmarkItemProcessor.java```
   - **Regression Assertion**: No record in analytics.benchmark_master or ```sql analytics.fund_master.benchmark_name``` may match ```text (?i)after[\s-]?tax(?:es)? or (?i)before[\s-]?tax(?:es)?```.

##### **BUG-BM-02**: XBRL Table Header & Schema Tag Leakage via Text Fallback
  - **Root Cause**: When numeric facts yielded no benchmark, Step 2 queried ```java findUnresolvedBenchmarksFromTextFacts```. 
  - Missing rawMeasure values caused the reader to fall back to ```java textSource.getStdLabel()```. 
  - Because Step 2 lacked the garbage check and isGenericBoilerplate only checked for spaced "table heading", raw schema elements (PerformanceTableHeading, IndexNoDeductionForFeesExpensesTaxes) were saved as benchmark records.   
  ```java BenchmarkItemProcessor.java```
  - **Regression Assertion**: No benchmark name may equal standard SEC XBRL tag identifiers ```text (PerformanceTableHeading, IndexNoDeductionForFeesExpensesTaxes, AverageAnnualReturnYear01, AvgAnnlRtrPct)```.

#####  **BUG-BM-03**: Filer Taxonomy Element Codes Leaked as Benchmark Names
  - **Root Cause**: Filers frequently define custom dimension members under ```text rr:PerformanceMeasureAxis``` in ```sql numeric_facts.measure``` without human-readable English titles in ```sql presentation_labels.terse``` or stdLabel. 
  - When labels were null, the reader pulled verbose or measure, staging raw taxonomy element IDs (```text SP001```, ```text LB001```, ```text MS001```, ```text RS001```, ```text DJ010```, ```text F0001``` through ```text F1905```).   
  ```java BenchmarkItemProcessor.java```
  - **Regression Assertion**: No benchmark name may match shorthand alphanumeric taxonomy patterns ```text ^[A-Z]{1,6}\d{1,5}(?:Member)?$```.

##### **BUG-BM-04**: SEC Filing Software Hash Codes and Auto-Numbered Tags
  - **Root Cause**: Software providers (Workiva, DFIN) inject autogenerated context hash tokens ```text(IX139, IX280, IXSLM, IXTOF, IXXXY)``` or generic numbered tags ```text (Index1 through Index102)```.
  - **Regression Assertion**: No benchmark name may match ```text ^IX[A-Z0-9]{3,5}$ or ^(?:Index\d+|[A-Z]{2,6}Index\d+)$```.

##### **BUG-BM-05**: Performance Table Footnote Dates & Percentage Returns Staged as Benchmarks
  - **Root Cause**: Footnote callout columns in performance tables contained dates (```text 5/28/2021```, ```text 3/2/2015```) and return percentages (```text 1.81%```, ```text 7.52%*```, ```text 9.04%**```), which were extracted as benchmark entities due to loose string constraints.
  - **Regression Assertion**: No benchmark name may consist solely of numeric/date values (```text ^\d{1,2}/\d{1,2}/\d{2,4}$ or ^[0-9.,%$* -]+$```).

##### **BUG-BM-06**: Non-Anchored Regex Discarding Legitimate Institutional Benchmarks
  - **Root Cause**: Adding unanchored sales\s+charge to the garbage pattern matched standard institutional prospectus disclaimers (e.g., ```text "S&P 500 Index (reflects no deductions for sales charges...)"```). Furthermore, ```java BenchmarkItemProcessor``` ran the check on rawCandidate before stripping disclaimer boilerplate, discarding valid benchmarks across 73 funds.   
  ```java BenchmarkItemProcessor.java```
  - **Regression Assertion**: Disclaimer boilerplate must be stripped prior to candidate validation, and fee table patterns (sales charge, 12b-1, fee waiver) must anchor to line beginnings.

##### **BUG-BM-07**: Outer Trailing $ Anchor Breaking Substring Garbage Rejections
  - **Root Cause**: Placing an outer $ anchor at the end of the non-capturing group ```text (?: ... )$``` forced every branch to match to the end of the string, preventing PerformanceTable from matching ```text PerformanceTableHeading``` and ```text after-tax``` from matching multi-word tax phrases.
  - **Regression Assertion**: Exclusion rules must evaluate individual branch boundaries without outer anchoring collisions.

##### **BUG-BM-08**: Cross-Series Benchmark Contamination within Multi-Series Filings (adsh)
  - **Root Cause**: For filings containing multiple series_id records under one ```sql accession_number```, the engine assigned benchmarks across series boundaries because candidate selection lacked composite (```text series_id```, ```text accession_number```) scoping and failed to cross-reference narrative text blocks.
  - **Regression Assertion**: Benchmark associations in fund_benchmark_association must strictly preserve (```text series_id```, ```text accession_number```) isolation.

##### **BUG-BM-09**: Unresolved Benchmark Orphan Mappings (benchmark_id = 0)
  - **Root Cause**: Funds with valid benchmark candidates in fund_benchmark_association were left unassigned (```text benchmark_id = 0```) in fund_master due to missing link passes.
  - **Regression Assertion**: 
```sql 
  SELECT COUNT(*) 
  FROM fund_benchmark_association fba 
  JOIN fund_master fm 
    ON fm.series_id = fba.series_id 
  WHERE fm.benchmark_id = 0 
    AND fba.benchmark_id <> 0 
    AND fba.is_primary = TRUE
``` 
must return exactly 0.

### 2. Strategy Classification Engine Bugs
##### **BUG-ST-01**: Active Management Terms Flagged as UNKNOWN
   - **Root Cause**: The compliance_rules table matched actively managed but lacked noun phrases (active management approach, active management strategy, active and frequent trading), causing active funds (e.g., Vanguard U.S. Growth Fund, T. Rowe Price Target Date series) to default to UNKNOWN.   
   ```java BenchmarkItemProcessor.java```
   - **Regression Assertion**: Funds containing "```text active management approach```", "```text actively selects```", or "```text bottom-up fundamental research```" must classify as ```text ACTIVE```.

##### **BUG-ST-02**: Inverted Tier Resolution Causing False Active Classification
   - **Root Cause**: Running narrative compliance rules before fund name heuristics caused passive index funds (e.g., ```text Vanguard 500 Index Fund```) to classify as ```text ACTIVE``` because prospectus narratives describe how index providers (e.g., ```text S&P```) "```text actively maintain and reconstitute```" the index.
   - **Regression Assertion**: Tier 1 Fund Name heuristics (```text Index Fund```, ```text Idx ETF```) must take precedence over general narrative text matching.
